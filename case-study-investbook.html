The challenge

Commercial real-estate opportunities arrive with inconsistent pricing, property details, broker information, and media. Investors need a fast way to search across that information, isolate affordable opportunities, preserve promising deals, and communicate with other participants.

The product also needed to work when network conditions are imperfect, protect subscription-only functionality, support account and privacy requirements, and package everything as a reliable iOS application.

The product we built

InvestBook combines discovery, evaluation, organization, and deal communication in one mobile workflow. Its navigation is centered on Dashboard, Opportunities, Deals, Portfolio, Under $200K, Chat, and Profile.

01

Opportunity discovery

Investors can browse commercial listings and businesses, search by keyword, and filter by location, property type, pricing, and disclosure status.

02

Affordable-deal workflow

A dedicated Under $200K experience separates lower-cost properties and businesses from the broader opportunity feed.

03

Detailed evaluation

Deal pages consolidate images, pricing, address, property facts, square footage, cap rate, broker details, and source links.

04

Investor portfolios

Portfolio services let users retain and organize opportunities instead of repeatedly rebuilding research from the main listing feed.

05

Real-time deal chat

Socket.IO communication supports direct and deal-specific conversations, participant lookup, message state, and notifications.

06

Native monetization

Monthly and yearly view-only and chat plans use Apple in-app purchases, with backend receipt verification and access guards.
