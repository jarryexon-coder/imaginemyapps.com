Frequently asked questions

Where are saved project briefs stored?

Project briefs are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to ImagineMyApps unless you share them or submit information through the consultation form.

Are planner estimates final quotes?

No. Planner results are directional ranges for early planning. Final pricing requires a review of design, technical, content, and integration requirements.

How can I delete a project?

Open My Projects in the app, select the project, then choose Delete Project.

How can I request deletion of consultation information?

Email admin@imaginemyapps.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request.”
