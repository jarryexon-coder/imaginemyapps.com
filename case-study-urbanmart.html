UrbanMart: E-commerce Simplified

An online shopping tool designed to help customers compare options and find lower prices.

Overview

UrbanMart brings product discovery, price comparison, and convenient shopping into one mobile-first experience. The project focuses on reducing the effort required to compare products and make a confident purchase.

The Challenge

Online shoppers often move between multiple stores and product pages to compare prices. UrbanMart organizes that fragmented journey into a clearer experience built around quick discovery and value.

Our Solution

  • Product discovery and category browsing
  • Price-focused comparison experience
  • Mobile shopping and package-delivery workflow
  • Clear product details and purchase actions
  • Responsive interface for phones and tablets

Why This Matters

UrbanMart demonstrates how thoughtful product design can simplify a familiar but frustrating customer journey and turn comparison shopping into a focused experience.
