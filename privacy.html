1. Information We Collect

When you use our consultation form, we collect:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your consultation requests

3. Data Storage

Your consultation information is stored using our database and hosting service providers. We retain consultation records only as long as needed to respond, provide requested services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion using the contact information below.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent consultation information. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the service, including database hosting, application hosting, email delivery, and advertising measurement providers. Those providers process information on our behalf.

We use the TikTok Pixel to measure visits and successful consultation submissions from TikTok advertising. The Lead event does not include the name, email address, phone number, or consultation message entered in the form.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@imaginemyapps.com

6. Cookies

We use first-party cookies and similar technologies for site operation and advertising attribution, including measuring whether a TikTok advertisement led to a visit or completed consultation request. You can limit or disable cookies in your browser settings, though doing so may reduce attribution accuracy.
