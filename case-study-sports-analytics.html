The challenge

The existing product covered a wide range of sports, data sources, prediction tools, and account workflows. The mobile app needed to make that depth usable on a small screen without presenting users with an overwhelming list of disconnected features.

It also needed secure account access, package-level entitlements, native App Store subscriptions, live API connectivity, and clear account-management paths that satisfy Apple’s platform requirements.

The product we built

We organized the experience around MLB, NFL, NBA, NCAA, and a Supercharged research tier. Each customer sees the sports and tools included with their active package, while the navigation stays compact and native to iOS.

01

Live sports data

Schedules, team context, rosters, player performance, projections, news, and prop lines are normalized from production Railway APIs.

Advanced research

Sortable analytics, projection comparisons, confidence filters, market direction, and player-level detail support faster investigation.

Parlay suggestions and combination scoring turn live prop signals into transparent, reviewable research—not unexplained black-box outputs.

Expo Application Services provides signed production builds, automatic build-number increments, TestFlight delivery, and repeatable releases.

How the system works

The mobile client stays lean by delegating identity, live sports aggregation, and purchase state to specialized services.

Expo + React NativeNative navigation, accessible screens, dark theme, and mobile interaction patterns.
FirebaseEmail authentication, stable user identity, and server-verifiable access tokens.
Railway APIsLive games, projections, props, news, player data, and account profiles.

What the build demonstrates

This project shows how a feature-heavy web platform can become a focused native product without duplicating backend logic or exposing provider credentials in the mobile bundle.

  • A native information architecture for multiple sports
  • Real production data instead of static mockups
  • Package-aware navigation and access control
  • App Store-compliant purchase and restore flows
  • Shared identity across web, mobile, and backend
  • A repeatable TestFlight and release workflow
